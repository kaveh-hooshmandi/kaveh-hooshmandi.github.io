The Autonomous Driving Simulation Laboratory provides a realistic simulation environment for the development, evaluation, and validation of intelligent and autonomous vehicle algorithms before real-world deployment.

The laboratory supports research and student projects in:

- Artificial Intelligence for Autonomous Vehicles
- Computer Vision and Perception
- LiDAR, Camera, and Sensor Fusion
- Localization and Mapping
- Path and Motion Planning
- Decision-Making Algorithms
- Vehicle Control Algorithms
- Obstacle Detection and Avoidance
- Traffic Scenario Analysis
- Autonomous Navigation
- ROS/ROS 2 Integration
- Simulation-to-Real Validation

CARLA is used as one of the main simulation platforms to create realistic urban driving scenarios and evaluate autonomous driving algorithms under different traffic, environmental, and operational conditions.
